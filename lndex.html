It started on Telegram, August 7th, 2023 — the most unlikely place, the most ordinary moment, and somehow the most extraordinary beginning. You were a stranger on the other side of a screen and I remember thinking, there's something about this person. Something warm, something real, something that made me want to keep talking. And so we did. Long messages, random thoughts, 2am conversations about nothing and everything all at once. Telegram was just the door, but you were the reason I walked through it.

Then we found our way to Snapchat — and the friendship grew its own heartbeat. Streaks became rituals. Snaps became little love letters. The silly ones, the random ones, the "look at this and think of you" ones. You showed up every single day without ever making it feel like an obligation. That's when I knew — this wasn't just a fun internet friendship. This was the real thing. You were the real thing.

And then came Instagram — and I think the universe exhaled. Like it had been holding its breath waiting for us to arrive here, waiting for the official chapter to begin. On Instagram we could see each other's worlds more fully — the photos, the reels, the aesthetic little corners of our lives. But more than the feed and the stories, what I remember most is how easy it was to love who you are. Publicly. Loudly. Without any hesitation.

Because here's what I've learned across every app, every message, every voice note — Nitisha, you are one of those rare people who makes the world around them softer just by being in it. You carry so much warmth in the simplest things — a reply at the right moment, a laugh that happens miles away but still fills a room, a way of caring that asks nothing in return. I've never once had to explain myself fully to you. You just get it. You just get me.

From Telegram to Snapchat to Instagram — from a stranger to a best friend to someone I genuinely cannot imagine this life without. You have been one of the most beautiful surprises this universe has given me, and I say that with my whole heart. The apps change. The platforms update. But the way I feel about you? That doesn't need WiFi. That doesn't need a notification. That just is — constant, steady, and completely full of love.

I love you so much, Nitisha. More than words on any screen can hold. More than any caption can carry. You are my favourite conversation, my favourite kind of chaos, and my most favourite person. Today, tomorrow, every version of forever. 💜
